# Research and papers

This repository contains preprints, supporting [Lean](https://lean-lang.org/) artifacts, and numerical scripts related to my research on statistics and mathematical finance. Each paper is indexed by its thematic group (e.g., "I2").

## Information, identification, and protection in stochastic-volatility markets

This section concerns the conditions under which knowledge of risk becomes usable.

| Index | Paper | Main idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I1 | [Leverage and Structural Information in Noisy Stochastic-Volatility Prices](manuscripts/I1/manuscript.tex) | Dependence can make hidden dynamics learnable |
| I2 | [Sharp Discrete Hedging Rates with a Prescribed Call](manuscripts/I2/manuscript.tex) | A completing instrument can still be an inefficient hedge |
| I3 | [Capital, Protection, and Information in Dispersion Underwriting](manuscripts/I3/manuscript.tex) | Capital creates choices through which information acquires value |
